# QA Findings

Owner: QA. One row per finding, as `QA-nnn | branch | priority | what | where | expected`. `branch` is `main` for findings against the contracts themselves (docs, `packages/core`, `packages/data/lib/src/api/`).

Priorities: **P1** data loss, wrong money, a security hole or a broken acceptance item (blocks the merge); **P2** a contract gap or contradiction that will make an implementation or a test guess; **P3** minor gap or wording that leaves a result undefined.

The central agent records the outcome of each finding under "Central outcomes", and QA closes a row there (the **QA status** column) once the contract and a scenario in `test/e2e/PLAN.md` agree. A row stays open when its resolution is incomplete or introduced a new problem, which is then logged as a new finding. QA-032 onwards have no outcome yet.
